# R-024 · Manifest status temporarily falls back to evidence status

## Context

This problem was recorded during `implement` for
`feature.concorde.define-project-ontology` and concerns `specs/concorde/modules/auto-docs/features/001-publish-project-docsite.md`.

## Expected

Build Manifest 10 feature records expose the requested `status` field with one unambiguous Profile 5 meaning.

## Observed

Profile 5 front matter retains `evidence_status`, while most concise feature designs no longer carry a separate body lifecycle Status; both concepts cannot be represented faithfully by one field.

## Impact

Recorded effect: assumed. Auto-Docs reads a legacy body Status when present and otherwise maps `evidence_status` into Manifest 10 `status`, documenting the temporary semantic overlap.

## Evidence

Primary concern: `specs/concorde/modules/auto-docs/features/001-publish-project-docsite.md`.

## Triage Analysis

Migrated triage record: the prior log identified the gap between the expected and observed behavior and recorded this workflow response: Auto-Docs reads a legacy body Status when present and otherwise maps `evidence_status` into Manifest 10 `status`, documenting the temporary semantic overlap.

## Proposed Resolution

Rename/split the field in Manifest 11 after consumers can migrate to explicit lifecycle and evidence fields.
