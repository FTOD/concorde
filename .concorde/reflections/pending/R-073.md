# R-073 · Foreign and superseded gaps pollute stage snapshots and never resolve

## Context

During the coordinated change `change.8325c8c8-747e-48e9-b06e-ccd226365307` (root target
`module.concorde`, components Development, Harness, Distribution, Reflections, Spec, Views), the
root Module's `tasks` stage was rejected twice in a row with
`child_blocked: concorde-tasks blocked: incompatible_handoff: gap provenance differs from the
admitted context`. No task result was persisted; the loop stopped at `phase tasks / status blocked`.

At that moment the change carried five open gaps in `gap_history`, all attributed to
`module.harness` or `module.distribution` and recorded under the Development coordination layer.
Every one of them had already been answered by an explicit Spec repair, but the host resolves a
gap only when the same target's assessment for the same task text and phase succeeds again, and
those assessments had not been re-run.

`workspace_context()` in `src/concorde/harness/change_worktree.py` copied the change's complete
open gap list into the `workspace.gaps` field of every stage snapshot, whatever the snapshot's
target. The root planner therefore wrote a plan item asking the host to "resolve the recorded
Harness ... questions", and the root task author restated those foreign gaps as its own output.
The host's provenance rule (`target_id` must equal the invocation's target and `context_id` must
equal the frozen snapshot) then rejected the whole result.

## Expected

A bounded worker sees only the gaps that belong to its own Module and its current task, because
those are the only gaps it can act on; the host carries every other Module's gap history itself.
A gap that a Spec repair has answered is resolved when the affected assessment succeeds again,
regardless of which coordination layer recorded it, and a gap whose task text was replaced by a
re-plan does not stay open forever. A planner or task author never has to reason about another
Module's blockers to produce an admissible result.

## Observed

- `workspace_context()` exposed all open gaps of the change to every stage snapshot. The frozen
  `workspace` observation is part of the planner's and task author's context, so cross-target gaps
  became visible instructions to them.
- Gaps are keyed by `(target_id, task, phase, question, blocked_step, needed_contract)` and
  resolved only by a later successful assessment with the same key. When Development re-planned
  its component tasks, the recorded Harness task text changed, so gap `65fe0905…` (recorded under
  the earlier task text) can never be resolved by any future assessment.
- The same Harness admission gap was recorded twice, once at the root coordination layer and once
  at the Development coordination layer; the root record was resolved by a later Harness specify
  while the Development record stayed open, because resolution is scoped to one layer's task text.
- Within the candidate, the developer applied a maintenance repair: `workspace_context(root, *,
  target_id=None, task=None)` returns, for a target-bound snapshot, only the open `gap_history`
  entries of that target and task; `resolve_context` and `recheck_context` pass the target and task
  through, while discovery snapshots keep observing every open gap. With that repair the root
  `tasks` stage was admitted on the next run and the Development Spec author recorded the behavior
  as `req.development.stage-gap-ownership` with three scenarios. That repair is not yet delivered.

## Impact

Before the repair, any coordinated change with an unresolved component gap could not pass its
coordinator's `tasks` stage, and a retry reproduced the failure. After the repair, stale gaps no
longer leak into worker contexts, but two design questions remain: a gap keyed to a superseded task
text stays open in `gap_history` and in the public reflections-triage `gap_records` forever, and a
gap recorded at one coordination layer is not resolved by the equivalent successful assessment at
another layer, so the change's gap list overstates its real blockers.

  digest over target, task, phase and the three gap fields; resolution only for the same key) and
  `#unchanged_task_gaps`.
- Snapshot exposure: `src/concorde/harness/change_worktree.py#workspace_context` at `569ac7ba`
  (`"gaps": state["gaps"]`) and the callers in `src/concorde/harness/context.py`.
- The candidate's repair and its Spec: `specs/concorde/development/review-and-gaps.md`, section
  "Gap isolation in stage snapshots", and `specs/concorde/harness/context.md`, workspace paragraph,
  both in the candidate worktree of change `8325c8c8`.

Candidate directions, for triage rather than decided here:

- Key gap resolution on the gap's own identity (target, phase and the three fields) rather than on
  the task text, or resolve a gap when the same Module's assessment for the same phase succeeds
  under any task text of the same change.
- Resolve a gap recorded at one coordination layer when the equivalent assessment succeeds at
  another layer, or record component gaps once per change instead of once per layer.
- Keep the delivered form of the snapshot repair: a target-bound snapshot carries only its own
  Module's gaps for its current task.
