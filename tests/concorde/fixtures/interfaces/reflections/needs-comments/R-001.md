# R-001 · Health-check timeout is unspecified

## Context

Planning the dependency health-check contract required a concrete timeout before tasks could be
written for degraded dependencies.

## Expected

FR-002 defines how long a dependency probe may take before the check reports degraded.

## Observed

No timeout is stated; fail-fast and wait-for-the-slowest readings lead to different contracts.

## Impact

Planning had to retain the ambiguity rather than silently choose product behavior.

## Evidence

`specs/example/modules/api/features/002-add-health-check.md#functional-requirements`

## Triage Analysis

The timeout is observable contract behavior and cannot be inferred from implementation detail.

## Proposed Resolution

Clarify FR-002 with an explicit dependency-probe budget.

## Intervention Rationale

A maintainer must choose the public timeout because no existing authority owns that value.

## User Comments

Use a 2-second budget.
